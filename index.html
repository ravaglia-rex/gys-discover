How the exam works

  1. It adjusts to your child

    Answer correctly and the next question gets a little harder. Miss one and it eases off. The exam spends its time where your child actually is.

  2. Harder questions count for more

    Two students with the same number of right answers can score differently. The score reflects the difficulty your child has mastered, not just the count.

  3. You see where they stand

    An 80th percentile means your child did as well as or better than 80% of GYS students at the same level. It is about standing, not "80% correct".

Questions parents ask

Is ₹299 a one-time payment?

Yes. Discovery is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If you upgrade to a full plan while Discovery is active, the ₹299 is credited toward it.

What is an adaptive exam?

An exam that adjusts as your child goes. It starts at a moderate level, then gets harder after correct answers and easier after misses, until it finds the level where your child is challenged but not overwhelmed.

Is a percentile the same as a percentage?

No. A percentile is your child's standing compared with other GYS students at the same level. An 80th percentile is not "80% correct"; it means they did as well as or better than 80% of that group.

Which classes is GYS for?

Classes 6–12. The exams adapt to each student's level.

What does my child need?

A phone or computer and a quiet stretch of time. There is nothing to install.
